📋 Instructions to be Followed

  1. Register and log in using your registered mobile number.
  2. Verify your identity using the OTP sent to your mobile.
  3. Read all instructions carefully before operating the Smart Waste Bin.
  4. Ensure you have a stable internet connection while using the application.
  5. Open the Smart Waste Bin only after successful login.
  6. Wait until the lid opens completely before placing waste.
  7. Dispose only biodegradable or permitted waste into the bin.
  8. Do not throw burning, hazardous, chemical, or sharp objects into the bin.
  9. Do not overload the Smart Waste Bin.
  10. Allow the lid to close automatically after disposing of the waste.
  11. Check the Live Bin Status before using the bin.
  12. View previous disposal activities in the History section.
  13. Use the Location section to locate the Smart Waste Bin when required.
  14. Keep the surroundings of the bin clean after use.
  15. Log out after completing your work and report any malfunction to the administrator.
